# Purpose

Define the primary user personas for the AI Career Interview Platform. These personas guide UX decisions, AI behavior, interview difficulty defaults, feedback style, and future feature planning.

---

# Persona 1 — Final-Year Student

**ID:** UP-001

## Profile
- Age: 20–23
- Education: Final-year undergraduate
- Experience: Internship or academic projects
- Goal: Campus placement

## Pain Points
- Little interview experience
- Nervous during technical interviews
- Doesn't know weak areas
- Limited access to mentors

## Needs
- Resume-based questions
- Beginner-friendly feedback
- Interview history
- Clear improvement roadmap

Referenced Features:
- FR-010, FR-020, FR-030, FR-046, FR-052

# AI Personalization Rules

The AI interview engine should adapt based on persona by adjusting:
- Question complexity
- Follow-up depth
- Feedback detail
- Encouragement level
- Suggested improvement roadmap
