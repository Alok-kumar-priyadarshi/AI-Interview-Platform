# Purpose

Define the long-term vision, business goals, and guiding principles for the AI Career Interview Platform. This document is the foundation for all future technical and product decisions.

---

# Vision Statement

Build an AI-powered interview preparation platform that provides realistic, personalized, voice-enabled mock interviews for students and job seekers, helping them improve interview performance through actionable feedback and continuous practice.

---

# Problem Statement

Many candidates struggle with interviews because they:
- Lack realistic interview practice.
- Do not receive structured feedback.
- Cannot identify weak technical or communication skills.
- Have no affordable AI interview coach available 24×7.

---

# Target Users

## Primary Users
- Final-year students
- Fresh graduates
- Job seekers
- Career switchers

## Secondary Users (Future)
- Colleges
- Placement cells
- Training institutes
- Recruiters

---

# Product Goals

1. Conduct realistic AI mock interviews.
2. Support voice and text interaction.
3. Generate personalized interview questions from resumes.
4. Adapt interview difficulty based on selected level and target salary/package.
5. Produce detailed performance reports.
6. Track interview history and user progress.
7. Remain easy to deploy and inexpensive to operate.

---

# Core Features (MVP)

- Google Login
- Resume Upload
- Resume Analysis
- Difficulty Selection
- Package-based Difficulty
- Voice Interview
- Text Interview
- Male/Female AI Voice
- AI Feedback
- Interview History
- Performance Dashboard
- Improvement Suggestions

---

# Product Principles

- Documentation-first development.
- SOLID architecture.
- Clean and modular code.
- Feature specifications before implementation.
- API-first backend design.
- Context persistence for AI-assisted development.
- Free or open technologies wherever practical.

---

# Success Metrics

Technical:
- Response latency under 3 seconds for standard interactions.
- Stable deployment.
- Modular architecture.

User:
- Complete interview sessions.
- Actionable feedback reports.
- Progress improvement over multiple sessions.

---

# Out of Scope (Version 1)

- Multi-language interviews
- Live interviewer collaboration
- Company-specific interview datasets
- Coding IDE execution environment
- Video interview analysis

These may be added in later versions.

---

# Future Vision

The platform will evolve into an AI Career Coach with:

- ATS Resume Review
- Company-specific interview preparation
- Coding interviews
- Behavioral interview coaching
- HR interview simulations
- Learning roadmap generation
- Recruiter analytics
- College placement dashboard
